Harbor Town Opens a Floating Library

The small harbor town of Westmere opened a library on a converted ferry on Saturday, and more than four hundred visitors came aboard on the first day.

The ferry was bought from the regional transport company for a symbolic price. Volunteers spent two winters removing the car deck and building shelves for about twelve thousand books.

The library will moor at a different village every week during the summer. In winter it stays in Westmere, where it doubles as a reading room with a view of the bay.

The town council expects the running costs to be covered by a small membership fee and by renting the upper deck for concerts and school trips.
