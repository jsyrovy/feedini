The Quiet Keyboard Company Ships Its First Model

A two-person workshop called Quiet Keyboard has shipped its first mechanical keyboard after a year of delays. The keyboard uses felt-lined switches that the founders designed themselves.

The first batch of nine hundred units sold out in an hour. A second batch is planned for the spring, with a numeric keypad that many buyers asked for.
Prices start at 149 euros.

What is inside

  • Felt-lined switches with a travel of 3.2 mm
  • An aluminium case made in a local foundry
  • Firmware that can be changed without special tools
The founders say they will publish the design files of the case once the second batch ships, so that others can make spare parts.
