Mountain Hut Gets a Solar Roof

The highest mountain hut in the Snowy Range no longer needs its diesel generator. Since September its roof has been covered with solar panels, and a battery in the old coal cellar keeps the lights on at night.

Hikers will notice the difference mainly in the quiet evenings. The generator used to run until ten o'clock and could be heard from the summit path.

The panels were carried up by a helicopter in two flights. The rest of the work was done by volunteers from the hiking club, who spent four weekends on the roof.
